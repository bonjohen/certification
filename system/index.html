Atlas · Design System

A quiet system for serious studying.

Atlas is a local study tool. Editorial type, restrained chrome, provider colors as accents. Light and dark, neither shouting. This page is the source of truth — anything in /quiz.html, /azure.html, or any other screen pulls from these tokens.
